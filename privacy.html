Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: October 21, 2026 • Aesthetic Haven Studio (agentwell.agency)

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Aesthetic Haven Studio (“we,” “our,” or “us”), accessible via our primary domain at https://agentwell.agency. We curate aesthetic living essentials, warm room inspiration, smart kitchen products, and digital planners.

We deeply respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information collected when you browse our website, interact with our Pinterest boards, download free resources, or click our curated recommendations. This Privacy Policy details our data collection practices, our affiliate disclosure, and your rights.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Voluntary Information: When you subscribe to our Sunday Edit newsletter or request downloadable aesthetic guides (such as our Lighting Guide or Mindful Weekly Planners), we collect your name and email address.
  • Automated Technical Information: As you navigate through our studio pages, our hosting provider and analytics may collect non-personally identifiable log information, including your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referral source, and visit timestamps.
  • Cookies & Storage: Small text files placed on your device to maintain site responsiveness, remember interface preferences, and attribute affiliate transactions.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business and editorial purposes:

  • Delivering the requested digital guides, templates, and seasonal moodboards to your inbox.
  • Operating, optimizing, and maintaining the speed, security, and stability of our website.
  • Analyzing visitor traffic trends to curate relevant home accents and organizational products.
  • Responding to your direct inquiries submitted to our curator desk.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal email or identity to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Affiliate Disclosures

Amazon Associates Program Disclosure

Aesthetic Haven Studio is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites.

Affiliate Cookie Tracking Notice: As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. When you click an Amazon link featured on Aesthetic Haven Studio, Amazon may place tracking cookies or tracking tags onto your device to attribute the referral and track purchases made within the designated referral window. This incurs no extra cost to you and ensures our independent curation remains freely accessible.

Pinterest Services & Bot Crawlers

Our platform interacts seamlessly with Pinterest (Pinterest, Inc.). We welcome Pinterest crawlers (Pinterestbot/1.0) and adhere to Pinterest Open Graph and Rich Pin standards.

When you visit our site from Pinterest boards, Pinterest may collect referral data and utilize tracking technologies subject to Pinterest’s Privacy Policy.

Hosting & Infrastructure

Our platform is deployed globally via Vercel Inc. and Google Cloud infrastructure. These providers process automated transmission data in accordance with their respective privacy and ISO-certified security protocols.

5. Cookie Policy & Your Choices

You have full control over cookies. You can configure your browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge) to refuse all cookies, reject specific third-party cookies, or alert you whenever a cookie is placed.

Note that disabling all cookies may slightly alter your browsing experience, although all product links, guides, and curated collections will remain fully viewable.

6. Data Protection & Retention

We implement industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.3) across all pages and API endpoints on agentwell.agency.

Newsletter subscriber data is retained only for as long as you wish to remain on our mailing list. Every newsletter communication includes a direct one-click unsubscribe mechanism.

7. Children’s Online Privacy

Aesthetic Haven Studio is intended for general design audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13, adhering to COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) requirements.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect evolving legal guidelines, new affiliate partnerships, or enhanced features. Any modifications will be posted immediately with a revised “Last Updated” date at the top of this document.
